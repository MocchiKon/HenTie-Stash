Title search index rebuilt.

Rewrites every tag, artist, character, parody, group and category name in lower case, which is the one spelling they are stored in. Run this for a library whose names were added before that was the case, or right after the one-time import from the old database. Two spellings of one name (Big Sister and big sister) are merged into a single item, so the chapters and series carrying either end up on the same one. Tags are also given the spelling imports use: female:halo becomes halo ♀, male:halo becomes halo ♂, and mixed:, other:, location: and temp: are dropped.
