Starts at the default mode from Settings. Applies to the images of the chapters queued by this form, and is remembered on each queued link — so changing it afterwards does not affect what is already waiting. Pages are downscaled and re-encoded as they download, and an image is kept as it is whenever the saving does not clear the mode's thresholds.

A new chapter whose full title is exactly that of a chapter from a different source (or of one added without a link) is not downloaded; it lands in the queue's Failed list naming the chapter it matched. Chapters from the same source are never compared. Remembered on each queued link — to let one through anyway, use its "Retry allowing duplicate title" button in the queue, or paste it again with this unticked.

Download all favourites

Queues every gallery in your favourites on that site that is not in the library yet, with the Image Compression mode and the duplicate-title choice above. A gallery downloaded before and deleted since is left out; paste its link to download it again.

The favourites list belongs to the owner of an API key: set the site's key in Settings first.
