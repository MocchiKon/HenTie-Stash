For e-hentai/exhentai paste the gallery link (/g/<gid>/<token>/), not a page's (/s/…); for chaika the archive link (/archive/<id>/), not the gallery's.

Starts at the default mode from Settings. Applies to the images of the chapters queued by this form, and is remembered on each queued link — so changing it afterwards does not affect what is already waiting. Pages are downscaled and re-encoded as they download, and an image is kept as it is whenever the saving does not clear the mode's thresholds.

A new chapter whose full title is exactly that of a chapter from a different source (or of one added without a link) is not downloaded; it lands in the queue's Failed list naming the chapter it matched. Chapters from the same source are never compared. Remembered on each queued link — to let one through anyway, use its "Retry allowing duplicate title" button in the queue, or paste it again with this unticked.

gallery-dl (hitomi, e-hentai/exhentai)

These sources are downloaded by gallery-dl. nhentai and chaika ignore these choices. They start at the defaults from Settings and are remembered on each queued link.

gallery-dl reads the cookies of the browser you are logged in with (--cookies-from-browser). For e-hentai/exhentai only: hitomi needs no login. With cookies, e-hentai galleries are downloaded from exhentai.org, or from e-hentai.org if your account has no ExHentai access. Firefox works best: on Windows, Chrome, Edge and Brave encrypt their cookies in a way gallery-dl cannot read.

e-hentai/exhentai only; hitomi never serves the originals. Unticked: the resampled image (usually 1280 px wide, or your account's "Image Size" setting), 1 point of your image limit per page. Ticked: the original file. It needs cookies of a logged-in account (without them the resampled image is used), costs 1 point plus 1 per 0.1 MB of the file (a 3 MB page costs 31), and can cost GP. The limit then runs out much sooner, and downloads fail with "Image limit exceeded" until it recovers or you reset it on e-hentai.

error

A number, or a range for a random wait, e.g. 0.4-0.65. e-hentai/exhentai: the wait before each request for an image page (--sleep-request; it replaces gallery-dl's own 3-6 seconds). hitomi: the wait before each image download (--sleep). Too low a value can get your IP temporarily banned (e-hentai bans for "excessive pageloads").

Download all favourites

Queues every gallery in your favourites on that site that is not in the library yet, with the choices above. A gallery downloaded before and deleted since is left out; paste its link to download it again.

The favourites list belongs to the owner of an API key: set the site's key in Settings first.
