problem

A subscription follows a search on nhentai, e-hentai or exhentai. It queues every gallery the search already has, newest first, and then looks for new ones. What it queues downloads after the links you paste. It never queues a gallery that is in the library, one you downloaded before (even if you deleted it since), or one already in the queue - a link you pasted keeps the choices you gave it.

Removing a gallery from the queue does not stop a re-check, or another subscription with a similar search, from queueing it again. Pause a subscription to stop it listing; what it queued still downloads.
